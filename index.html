Letters

Give a letter its own color. Tick Pegs to print it separately: it gets peg holes, and the background gets matching pegs. That way the main print doesn't swap filament on every layer for one letter.

Separate (pegged) letters are placed face-up, with the peg holes on the bed. The holes are small enough to bridge, so no supports are needed.
